`robust-mixed-dist` is a Python package that implements robust statistical distances for mixed-type multivariate data (numerical, ordinal, nominal and binary variables), developed as part of my PhD research on distance-based learning methods for complex, large-scale data.

The package underpins the robust distances introduced in Grané &amp; Scielzo-Ortiz (2025), *"On generalized Gower distance for mixed-type data: extensive simulation study and new software tools"*, published in SORT - Statistics and Operations Research Transactions ([DOI: 10.57645/20.8080.02.28](https://doi.org/10.57645/20.8080.02.28)).
