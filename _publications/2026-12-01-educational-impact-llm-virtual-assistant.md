This study presents a controlled trial with 191 upper secondary and vocational students examining whether an LLM-based pedagogical agent, combining a consultative chat, a proactive evaluative chat and automatically generated study materials, improves learning from instructional video. Access to the agent alone did not raise average learning gains; instead, outcomes depended on the cognitive depth of student-AI interaction, measured through a novel Weighted Semantic Depth Index derived from chat logs. The agent narrowed rather than widened prior-attainment gaps. **Status: submitted for publication.**
