This paper presents a robust computational framework to map global public opinion and ideological polarization on the Gaza conflict, using Reddit data. The pipeline integrates large-scale data engineering, LLM-based zero-shot feature extraction with human-in-the-loop validation, and a robust mixed-data clustering framework, uncovering a bidimensional structure of digital polarization: a horizontal ideological axis (pro-Israel vs. pro-Palestine) and a vertical deliberative-affective axis (analytical/emotional vs. hostile/reactive). **Status: submitted for publication.**

Presented as an oral communication at the [XLII National Congress in Statistics, Operations Research and Data Science (SEIO 2026)]({{ "/talks/2026-09-01-gaza-polarization-seio-santiago" | relative_url }}), Santiago de Compostela, September 2026.
